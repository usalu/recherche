# Patrick Teuffel — relevance to reuse

## Reuse relevance at a glance

Patrick Teuffel is one of the most directly relevant people in this set for **structural reuse**. His work is strongly tied to circular structural design, especially the reuse of load-bearing components such as precast concrete elements.

His reuse relevance is not just theoretical. Circular Structural Design states that the EU-funded **ReCreate** project addresses the potential for reusing precast concrete elements, and that CIRCULAR STRUCTURAL DESIGN is an industry partner leading **Work Package 5: Redesign and Reassembly**. This places Teuffel’s work at the point where recovered structural components are turned back into design inputs for new buildings.

## Why his work matters for reuse

Reuse in structural engineering is difficult because load-bearing components cannot simply be treated like generic second-hand materials. They must be assessed for geometry, damage, connection details, load capacity, safety, regulations, logistics, and design compatibility. Teuffel’s work focuses on making this technically and professionally feasible.

His contribution is especially relevant in three areas:

1. **Redesign around existing components**  
   Instead of designing a building first and ordering new components, reuse projects often start with a stock of available elements. Teuffel’s circular structural design approach helps reverse the design process: the recovered component becomes a constraint and a resource.

2. **Reassembly of reused structural elements**  
   Reused precast concrete components need new structural configurations, new connection concepts, and safety verification. This is central to ReCreate’s redesign and reassembly agenda.

3. **Moving reuse from pilot to practice**  
   Teuffel’s public work connects research, industry pilots, design guidelines, and education. That is important because reuse only scales if engineers, clients, contractors, and regulators can follow repeatable workflows.

## Link to ReCreate

ReCreate aims to demonstrate the deconstruction and reuse of intact precast structural components from condemned buildings in new buildings. Teuffel’s relevance is strongest here: he is connected to the design side of making those reused components work in new structures.

## Best way to describe him in a reuse-focused project

> Patrick Teuffel is a circular structural design specialist whose work is highly relevant to the reuse of load-bearing building components, especially through ReCreate’s redesign and reassembly of precast concrete elements.
