# Désirée / Desiree Mann — relevance to reuse

## Reuse relevance at a glance

Désirée / Desiree Mann is relevant to reuse through public DB InfraGO and Deutsche Bahn circular-construction work, especially **material passports**, **reuse-potential assessment**, and **circular strategies for railway real-estate assets**.

Madaster Germany identifies Desiree Mann as a **Dipl.-Ing. architect in building construction at DB InfraGO AG**. In the same Madaster event summary, DB InfraGO is described as aiming for climate neutrality by 2040 and introducing circular-economy methods using Madaster as a digital planning tool. The stated goals include material passports, identifying valuable materials, reducing raw-material consumption, and lowering CO2 emissions.

## Why her work matters for reuse

Reuse at infrastructure-owner scale depends on more than individual pilot projects. A large owner such as Deutsche Bahn / DB InfraGO needs repeatable ways to document assets, audit buildings before demolition, identify reusable components, and connect those components to future demand.

Mann’s reuse relevance is strongest in these areas:

1. **Material passports for reuse**  
   Material passports help preserve information about products and components so that they can be reused later rather than treated as anonymous waste.

2. **Reuse in large asset portfolios**  
   DB InfraGO manages many railway-related buildings and transport facilities. Reuse becomes more powerful when a portfolio owner can match components from one project to another.

3. **Pilot projects and standards**  
   Public Madaster material connects her to “Pilotprojekte Hochbau & Baustandards Verkehrsanlagenplanung,” meaning reuse is being explored not only as a one-off idea but as part of planning standards and pilot implementation.

4. **Mühldorf station case-study context**  
   Public conference material refers to a paper titled **“A Circular Strategy for Deutsche Bahn’s Real Estate Assets”** and describes a scalable reuse strategy using Mühldorf station as a case study. Mann is listed among the DB InfraGO co-authors in public excerpts and conference/proceedings material.

## Link to reuse practice

Her work is especially relevant to the operational side of reuse: how a major asset owner can identify reusable materials and components early enough to influence planning, procurement, audits, and future matching between projects.

## Best way to describe her in a reuse-focused project

> Désirée / Desiree Mann is relevant to reuse through DB InfraGO’s circular-construction work on material passports, pilot projects, and scalable reuse strategies for railway real-estate assets.
