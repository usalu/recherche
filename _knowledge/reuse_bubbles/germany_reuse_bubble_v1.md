# Germany reuse bubble: evidence-backed map of reclaimed-material infrastructure

**Version:** evidence-audited, 2026-06-05  
**Scope:** Germany-based actors, tools, hubs, standards, and building/district cases that support reclaimed-material reuse, urban mining, secondary-material circulation, or building-resource documentation.  
**Evidence rule:** every factual claim below is either footnoted, explicitly labelled as an interpretation from cited sources, or placed in a caution note. Claims from company pages are marked as **self-reported** unless supported by an independent or public-sector source.

---

## 1. Core thesis

Germany’s reuse ecosystem is best read as a **distributed infrastructure**, not as one single physical depot. The main layers are: **Concular/restado/Concular Shop** as a digital market and implementation layer; **Haus der Materialisierung, Material Mafia, Kunst-Stoffe and Bauteilbörsen** as a civic/physical-material layer; **DIN SPEC 91484, DIN SPEC 91525, DGNB Building Resource Passport, Madaster Germany and Urban Mining Index** as a standards/data layer; and **Berlin TXL, Heidelberg Circular City, Rathaus Korbach, ICE City Erfurt and other case projects** as test sites for district-scale, municipal, or project-scale application.[^concular-home][^restado-ecesp][^concular-shop][^hdm-info][^material-mafia][^bauteilboerse-bremen][^bauteilboerse-hannover][^din91484-din][^din91525-dinmedia][^dgnb-passport][^madaster-de][^umi-fh][^txl-concular][^heidelberg-city][^korbach-interreg][^ice-erfurt]

**Interpretation:** this is “infrastructural” because the actors do more than design isolated objects: they create repeatable processes for identifying, documenting, storing, selling, specifying, insuring, or reusing materials. That reading is inferred from the documented roles of Concular, restado, Concular Shop, HdM, Bauteilbörsen, DIN/DGNB/Madaster tools, and city/district pilots.[^concular-home][^concular-urban-mining][^restado-gtb][^concular-shop][^hdm-info][^tu-hdm][^din91484-din][^dgnb-passport][^madaster-de][^heidelberg-city]

### 4.3 Standards, data and legitimacy layer

| Tool / institution | What is evidenced | Network role | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DIN SPEC 91484** | DIN says DIN SPEC 91484 serves as a guide for pre-demolition audits and structures the process into preliminary and detailed examinations.[^din91484-din] GlobalABC summarizes it as a procedure to record building materials to evaluate high-quality reutilization potential before demolition and renovation.[^din91484-globalabc] | Standardizes the inventory/audit step before demolition or renovation. | **A** |
| **DIN SPEC 91525** | DIN Media lists DIN SPEC 91525:2026-02 as “Post-Use Concept (PUC) for construction products from existing buildings.”[^din91525-dinmedia] University of Kassel summarizes it as general principles for post-use concepts for construction products from existing buildings, focusing on technical, ecological and economic potential for high-quality post-use.[^din91525-kassel] | Bridges audit data and post-use planning; turns identified potential into a reuse/recycling decision framework. | **A** |
| **DGNB Building Resource Passport** | DGNB describes the Building Resource Passport as a documentation format creating an information base across the building life cycle and transparency about materials, greenhouse-gas emissions and circularity.[^dgnb-passport] | Documentation format for circularity and material transparency. | **A** |
| **Madaster Germany** | Madaster Germany says its platform creates building-resource passports from BIM/IFC or Excel data and adds circularity, sustainability and financial-value information.[^madaster-de] | Digital material-bank and resource-passport platform. | **B** |
| **Urban Mining Index** | FH Münster describes the Urban Mining Index as a system for quantitatively assessing the recycling potential of building structures in new-construction planning.[^umi-fh] Gebäudeforum says Prof. Anja Rosen developed it with support from Prof. Annette Hillebrandt at Bergische Universität Wuppertal as a planning instrument for assessing loop potential / subsequent-use capacity.[^umi-gebaeudeforum] | Measurement framework for circular construction potential. | **A/B** |
| **re!source Stiftung e.V.** | re!source describes itself as an independent alliance founded in 2018 by construction and real-estate-sector actors to advance sustainable resource use and circular value creation in building materials.[^resource-stiftung] | Sector alliance / advocacy and coordination layer. | **B** |

## 5. Buildings, hubs and case studies inside the German bubble

### 5.1 Harder evidence: direct reuse, urban mining, district-scale or documented material-passport work

## 6. How the German network connects

### 6.1 Concular chain

```text
Donor building
→ pre-deconstruction audit / digital inventory
→ material quality and post-use assessment
→ reuse matching, shop or direct transfer
→ selective deconstruction and logistics
→ receiving project
→ building-resource passport / future documentation
```

**Evidence:** Concular’s service pages describe circular construction, urban mining, component reuse, PDA according to DIN SPEC 91484, material inventories, reuse marketing, LCA and building-resource passports; Concular Shop describes documented/check/insured products from deconstruction; and Concular project pages document this chain in Leipzig Airport, Französische Straße, ICE City Erfurt, Forum Königsbrunn and Hangweide.[^concular-home][^concular-urban-mining][^concular-shop][^leipzig-concular][^franzoesische-strasse][^ice-erfurt][^forum-koenigsbrunn][^hangweide]

### 6.2 Berlin civic-material chain

```text
Haus der Statistik / Haus der Materialisierung
→ material cycles for residual and used materials
→ workshops, repair, lending, education and events
→ Material Mafia and other material initiatives
→ Berlin Re-Use Strategy / TU Berlin real-world lab
→ secondary-material practice for civic, creative and small-scale uses
```

**Evidence:** HdM documents material cycles, workshops and reuse/repair initiatives; HdM’s info page links the center to Koop5, the Berlin Re-Use Strategy and Kunst-Stoffe; TU Berlin frames HdM as a real-world laboratory; Hans Sauer Stiftung documents a secondary-material market and workshops; and Material Mafia / external reporting document a residual-material warehouse and redistribution role.[^hdm-home][^hdm-info][^tu-hdm][^hans-sauer-hdm][^material-mafia][^urbanactivist-materialmafia]

### 6.3 Standards/data chain

```text
DIN SPEC 91484
→ pre-demolition / pre-deconstruction audit
→ DIN SPEC 91525
→ post-use concept for products from existing buildings
→ DGNB / Madaster resource passports
→ transparency about material, value, emissions, circularity and future reuse
```

**Evidence:** DIN and GlobalABC describe DIN SPEC 91484; DIN Media and University of Kassel describe DIN SPEC 91525; DGNB describes the Building Resource Passport; and Madaster Germany describes generation of resource passports from BIM/IFC or Excel data.[^din91484-din][^din91484-globalabc][^din91525-dinmedia][^din91525-kassel][^dgnb-passport][^madaster-de]

```text
City, district developer or large portfolio owner
→ material registry / urban-mining strategy
→ repeat audits and documentation
→ pilots in public or portfolio assets
→ reusable procurement and planning routines
```

**Evidence:** Berlin TXL documents a long-term circularity framework with Concular; Heidelberg documents a city building-material registry/urban-mining pilot; Concular describes a Deutsche Bahn urban-mining strategy; and the National Circular Economy Strategy’s construction/buildings action area emphasizes circular approaches such as continued use, conversion, expansion, and digital documentation of circular construction.[^txl-concular][^txl-entwicklungsstadt][^heidelberg-city][^heidelberg-city-municipal][^db-concular][^nkws-construction]

## 8. Cautions: details not to overclaim

1. **Reuse vs. recycling:** Rathaus Korbach is a very strong urban-mining precedent, but the evidence mainly concerns mineral material recovery/reuse/recycling in concrete, backfill and subgrade, not direct reinstallation of whole building components.[^korbach-hessen][^korbach-umi]

2. **Company-reported success stories:** ICE City Erfurt, Forum Königsbrunn, Französische Straße, Hangweide, Behnisch München and parts of Leipzig Airport are currently evidenced mostly by Concular pages. Treat exact quantities, CO₂ figures and project roles as **Concular-reported** unless corroborated by project-owner, architect, contractor or municipality documents.[^ice-erfurt][^forum-koenigsbrunn][^franzoesische-strasse][^hangweide][^behnisch][^leipzig-concular]

3. **Data infrastructure is not the same as material reuse:** Huthmacher-Haus, MINT-Zentrum and Heidelberg Circular City strongly evidence material-passport or registry work. They should not be described as reclaimed-material construction projects unless additional evidence shows actual reused components or secondary materials installed.[^huthmacher-madaster][^mintzentrum][^heidelberg-city]

4. **Germany is distributed, not centralized:** no cited source proves a single national reuse hub equivalent to Rotor DC. The “bubble” reading is a synthesis from multiple sourced nodes: Concular/restado/shop, HdM/Material Mafia/Bauteilbörsen, DIN/DGNB/Madaster/UMI, and city/district pilots.[^concular-home][^hdm-info][^bauteilboerse-bremen][^din91484-din][^madaster-de][^heidelberg-city]
