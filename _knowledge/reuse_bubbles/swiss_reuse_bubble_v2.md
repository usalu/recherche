# The Swiss Reuse Bubble: Evidence-Backed Map of Infrastructural Reuse

**Working note**  
**Date:** 2026-06-05  
**Focus:** Swiss actors, tools, platforms, and projects that make reclaimed building-material reuse infrastructural rather than one-off.

---

## How to read this file

This version adds evidence for each factual claim. I use two labels:

- **Fact:** directly supported by a cited source.
- **Interpretive conclusion:** my analytical reading, based on the listed facts. These are not presented as statements from the sources themselves.

Where possible, the evidence is placed immediately after the claim. A source register is included at the end.

## 1. Core argument

**Interpretive conclusion:** The Swiss reuse scene is best understood as a **reuse bubble** or **reuse ecology**, rather than as a loose list of isolated projects.

**Evidence for this interpretation:**

| Evidence | Source |
|---|---|
| Cirkla describes itself as **“The Swiss re-use network”** and presents experts, documentation, and concrete projects. | [Cirkla homepage](https://www.cirkla.ch/en/) |
| Cirkla lists reuse actors across Switzerland, including companies, experts, architects, recycling centers, universities, private individuals, and projects. | [Cirkla reuse network](https://www.cirkla.ch/en/le-reseau-du-reemploi/) |
| Cirkla’s committee includes people from practice, platforms, universities, and reuse organizations, including baubüro in situ, Materiuum, Salza, Öbu, Losinger Marazzi, ETH Zurich, SUPSI, EPFL, ROTO-Reuse, and Berner Fachhochschule. | [Cirkla association](https://www.cirkla.ch/en/lassociation-cirkla/) |
| Zirkular combines circular-building specialist planning, building screening, competitions, research, teaching, Planular®, and reMATERIAL®. | [Zirkular services](https://zirkular.net/en/service/) |
| ETH Zurich describes SWIRCULAR as a project to create a digital ecosystem for circular construction in Switzerland, connecting universities, digital tools, data, legal/economic frameworks, and implementation partners. | [ETH Zurich on SWIRCULAR](https://ethz.ch/en/news-and-events/eth-news/news/2024/12/timber-earth-and-a-digital-ecosystem-for-sustainable-construction.html) |

**Interpretive definition used in this file:** A project or actor is **infrastructural** when it makes reuse easier for future projects by creating tools, supply chains, legal frameworks, data systems, marketplaces, inventories, training, or repeatable design methods.

This definition is an analytical framework, not a direct quote from one source. It is built from the evidence that Swiss actors are working on inventories, catalogues, component matching, legal guides, digital material passports, platforms, education, and network coordination.

**Fact:** Zirkular describes **K.118 in Winterthur** and **ELYS in Basel** as pioneering circular-building projects completed simultaneously by baubüro in situ in 2021.  
**Evidence:** The K.118 project page states that K.118 and ELYS, completed by baubüro in situ in 2021, proved to be pioneering projects of circular building.  
**Source:** [Zirkular K.118](https://zirkular.net/en/project/building-k-118/)

**Fact:** Zirkular states that K.118 and ELYS triggered the foundation of Zirkular by the involved planners.  
**Evidence:** The K.118 project page says the projects “were the trigger for the foundation of Zirkular.”  
**Source:** [Zirkular K.118](https://zirkular.net/en/project/building-k-118/)

**Fact:** K.118 involved the upward expansion of an existing industrial hall by three additional stories.  
**Evidence:** The K.118 page states that an existing industrial hall was expanded upwards with three additional stories.  
**Source:** [Zirkular K.118](https://zirkular.net/en/project/building-k-118/)

**Fact:** The K.118 project aimed to preserve the existing building and use as many components as possible from demolition projects.  
**Evidence:** The K.118 page states this aim directly.  
**Source:** [Zirkular K.118](https://zirkular.net/en/project/building-k-118/)

**Fact:** Zirkular states that, in K.118, a building structural system was reused through steel girders obtained from partial deconstruction of a hall in the ELYS project in Basel.  
**Evidence:** The K.118 page states this.  
**Source:** [Zirkular K.118](https://zirkular.net/en/project/building-k-118/)

**Fact:** Zirkular says the K.118 experience created a well-documented beginning for the new firm, from component search and design through component management, storage, and construction.  
**Evidence:** The K.118 page states that this process created “a rich and well-documented wealth of experience” and “a solid beginning for the new firm.”

**Interpretive conclusion:** K.118 is not only a building precedent but a method-generating case for the Swiss reuse bubble.

### 5.7 Material-flow chain

**Interpretive conclusion:** These actors make reuse practical by covering intermediate steps between donor buildings and receiving projects.

Evidence-backed chain:

```text
Donor building
→ identification / screening
→ component catalogue or inventory
→ potential assessment
→ removal / brokerage / storage
→ listing or marketplace publication
→ matching with a receiving building
→ reuse in a new project
```

| Chain step | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Identification / screening | Zirkular’s building screening records and evaluates building components. |
| Component catalogue or inventory | Zirkular creates detailed component catalogues via Planular®; Planular creates digital product passes. |
| Potential assessment | Zirkular assesses reuse potential; Planular supports condition and potential analysis. |
| Removal / brokerage / storage | Zirkular says it assists with component brokerage, coordinated removal, and reinstallation; Planular tracks storage; reMATERIAL includes dismantled and stored components. |
| Listing / marketplace | Salza, useagain, Bauteilladen, Cirkla-Scan, and reMATERIAL all provide different listing, search, shop, or marketplace functions. |
| Matching | Planular manages supply/demand matching; Zirkular describes matching supply and demand of components. |
| Reuse in a receiving project | Zirkular describes receiving buildings; K.118 documents reuse of components from demolition projects. |

**Sources:** [Zirkular services](https://zirkular.net/en/service/); [Planular](https://planular.net/); [Cirkla-Scan](https://www.cirkla.ch/en/cirkla-scan/); [Salza](https://salza.ch/); [useagain](https://www.useagain.ch/de/); [Bauteilladen Winterthur](https://bauteilladen.ch/); [Zirkular K.118](https://zirkular.net/en/project/building-k-118/)

## 10. How the Swiss bubble makes reuse infrastructural

**Interpretive conclusion:** The Swiss reuse bubble covers much of the operational chain required for reclaimed-material reuse.

Evidence-backed loop:

```text
1. Donor building appears
2. Components are inventoried or screened
3. Legal / technical feasibility is assessed
4. Components are dismantled, brokered, stored, or reserved
5. Components are listed, scanned, or managed through platforms
6. Designers search, match, and specify reused components
7. Receiving building integrates components
8. Material passports / documentation preserve future value
9. Knowledge feeds back into platforms, guides, networks, training, and research
```

| Step | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Zirkular building screening records/evaluates components; Planular supports initial recording before deconstruction; Swiss Inv inventories reuse potential. |
| 3 | Zirkular’s legal-framework project addresses contracts, permits, approval procedures, transport, storage, and legal gaps. |
| 4 | Zirkular assists component brokerage, coordinated removal, storage, and reinstallation; reMATERIAL handles dismantled and stored components. |
| 5 | Cirkla-Scan, Salza, useagain, Bauteilladen, Matériuum, and reMATERIAL provide platform/shop/resourcerie access. |
| 6 | Planular manages supply/demand matching and receiving buildings; Zirkular supports design and planning with suitable components. |
| 7 | K.118 reused components from demolition projects and steel girders from the ELYS partial deconstruction. |
| 8 | Madaster creates material passports and records data for future reuse after disassembly. |
| 9 | Cirkla disseminates knowledge through events and tools; Zirkular participates in research and teaching; SWIRCULAR links digital tools, data, legal/economic frameworks, demonstrators, and partners. |

**Sources:** [Zirkular services](https://zirkular.net/en/service/); [Planular](https://planular.net/); [Cirkla publications & tools](https://www.cirkla.ch/en/publications-outils/); [Zirkular legal framework project](https://zirkular.net/en/project/innosuisse-reuse-of-building-components-a-legal-framework/); [Cirkla-Scan](https://www.cirkla.ch/en/cirkla-scan/); [Salza](https://salza.ch/); [useagain](https://www.useagain.ch/de/); [Bauteilladen Winterthur](https://bauteilladen.ch/); [Matériuum](https://materiuum.ch/); [Zirkular K.118](https://zirkular.net/en/project/building-k-118/); [Madaster Switzerland](https://madaster.ch/en/); [ETH Zurich on SWIRCULAR](https://ethz.ch/en/news-and-events/eth-news/news/2024/12/timber-earth-and-a-digital-ecosystem-for-sustainable-construction.html)

## 13. Key takeaway

**Interpretive conclusion:** The Swiss case shows reclaimed-material reuse as an emerging infrastructure, not only as an architectural style.

**Evidence basis:**

- Cirkla provides national network visibility, directory functions, events, and tools.
- baubüro in situ and K.118 provide built precedents.
- Zirkular turns reuse into services, screening, component hunting, teaching, Planular®, and reMATERIAL®.
- Salza, useagain, Matériuum, Bauteilladen, Cirkla-Scan, and reMATERIAL® make materials findable, listable, storable, sellable, or specifiable.
- Madaster and Planular® provide data and material-management infrastructure.
- SWIRCULAR develops a digital ecosystem for circular construction in Switzerland.
- The Innosuisse/ZHAW/Zirkular/baubüro in situ legal-framework project addresses contractual and approval barriers.
- Circular Hub, C33, and Circular Economy Switzerland create a broader coordination and knowledge environment.

This is why the Swiss reuse bubble can be categorized as **infrastructural reclaimed-material reuse** in a reuse/scalability matrix.
