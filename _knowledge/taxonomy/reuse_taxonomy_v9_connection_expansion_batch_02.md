## Main changes by project
- **BlueCity Offices Rotterdam:** Separated window-frame partitions, reused steel, original concrete blocks and oil-platform balustrades with source/location/method edges.
- **Brent Cross Town Primary Substation:** Mapped reclaimed oil/gas tubular steel to stockholder procurement, testing/CE marking, availability-led design and carbon/reuse assessment.
- **CascadeUp / London secondary-timber glulam demonstrator:** Mapped secondary timber to glulamST and CLST products, with source, external location, scouting and remanufacturing edges.
- **Ferme du Rail Paris:** Mapped reused tiles, metallic elements, stones and recycled fibre/textile streams; added dry-connection/modular design and material-shape adaptation.
- **Hastings Pier Visitor Centre / reclaimed timber cladding:** Mapped same-site fire-damaged hardwood recovery into cladding and furniture with availability-based design and splitting/cutting preparation.
- **Liander / Alliander HQ, Duiven:** Separated retained building stock, waste-wood facades, demolished concrete, reused steel, recycled asphalt, toilets, ceiling plates and door-to-furniture transformation; added raw-material passport and disassembly design.
- **Lo-Reninge Town Hall façade / Stadhuis Lo:** Mapped reclaimed yellow brick and recycled paper insulation; added design selection and lime-mortar/slurry surface treatment.
- **Maison Vignette, Auderghem:** Resolved reused facing bricks and broad reclaimed/recycled material package; flagged weak preparation edge.
- **Maison des Canaux, Paris:** Expanded from broad reclaimed-material category to furniture, floors, tiles, doors/windows-to-decking, archive furniture and lighting; added audit, work sheets, careful dismantling, storage and technical validation.
- **Multi Brussels / Reuse in MULTI:** Expanded Rotor’s six reused batches into component-specific rows with own/external sources, locations, dismantling and dimensioning/upcycling preparation.
- **Upcycle Studios Copenhagen:** Expanded concrete, repurposed double-glazing and discarded flooring-board streams; mapped sources and remanufacturing.
