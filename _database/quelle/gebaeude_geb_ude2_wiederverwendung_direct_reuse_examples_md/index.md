# gebaeude_geb_ude2_wiederverwendung_direct_reuse_examples_md

This batch adds further **REUSE / Wiederverwendung** precedents, prioritising **complex built reuse**, **structural relevance**, **envelope relevance**, and documented design → sourcing → testing/refabrication → construction chains. I avoided repeating the main examples already captured in the previous lists unless a project appears here as a useful status/source correction.

## Rating logic used here

- **★★★★★** = complex built reuse, structural/envelope relevance, documented design → sourcing → build chain, built or essentially built.
- **★★★★☆** = strong built reuse with structural/envelope relevance, but smaller scale, older/less architectural documentation, or built status requires final verification.
- **★★★☆☆** = built reuse with genuine component reuse, but mostly small building, pilot, façade, service building, or limited public documentation.
- **★★☆☆☆** = pavilion, prototype, research/demonstrator, proposal, competition, or non-building infrastructure reuse.
- **★☆☆☆☆** = very small built reuse relevance.

## Recommended placement in the master list

1. Add **Timber Square** to the upper four-star band, near **Holbein Gardens**, **55 Great Suffolk Street**, **Brent Cross Town substation** and other UK reclaimed-steel projects.
2. Add **Udden student apartments**, **Nya Udden**, **Five Cottbus urban houses**, **Gröditz association house**, **Plauen association house** and **Mühlhausen multi-housing** to the four-star band because they show significant **built structural concrete-component reuse**.
3. Add **Big Dig House** to the four-star band: it is small, but its structural reuse is unusually clear and high-impact.
4. Add the remaining German/Finnish concrete-panel houses and service buildings to the three-star band, ordered by quantity and building relevance.
5. Keep **Plattenpalast**, **Plattenvereinigung**, **Superlocal exhibition building**, **Big Dig Building** and infrastructure/agricultural examples in the two-star band because they are prototypes, pavilions, proposals or non-building reuse.

## Caveats / verification tasks

- The German and Swedish precast-concrete examples are well documented in the 77-precedent concrete-reuse review, but many need additional primary-source verification for architects, engineers and exact current status.
- **Timber Square** is highly relevant but should be rechecked for final handover/completion documentation before upgrading to five stars.
- **London Olympic Stadium** should be treated as “verify” until the precise role and origin of the reused gas pipes is confirmed in project-level sources.
