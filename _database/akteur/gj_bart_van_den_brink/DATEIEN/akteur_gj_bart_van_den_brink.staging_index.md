# G.J. / Bart van den Brink — relevance to reuse

## Reuse relevance at a glance

G.J. van den Brink, also referred to as Bart van den Brink in related TU/e event material, is directly relevant to reuse through his master’s thesis on **reusing structural precast concrete elements** from disassembled office buildings in new apartment buildings.

The TU/e thesis record gives the title as: **“Designing with recovered precast concrete elements: a study on the possibilities of reusing structural precast concrete elements, from disassembled office buildings, in new apartment buildings.”** That title alone makes his reuse relevance very clear: the work studies whole-component reuse, not only recycling or material recovery.

## Why his work matters for reuse

Van den Brink’s research addresses one of the core questions of structural reuse:

> Can structural precast concrete elements taken from an existing building be used again in a new building type?

This matters because office buildings and residential buildings have different layouts, grids, load requirements, fire/acoustic expectations, façade requirements, and services. Reusing structural components across those building types requires careful matching between old component stock and new design demand.

His work is relevant in three practical ways:

1. **Component-to-building matching**  
   Reuse requires matching recovered beams, slabs, columns, or panels to a new design. Van den Brink’s topic focuses on whether elements from offices can become useful parts of apartment buildings.

2. **Higher-value reuse than recycling**  
   Reusing whole precast elements preserves much more embodied value than crushing concrete into aggregate. This aligns with ReCreate’s goal of keeping concrete at a high level of utilization.

3. **Foundation for later research**  
   Later work on recovered precast concrete elements cites or builds on the 2020 thesis. This suggests the thesis helped establish a practical basis for design-with-recovered-elements studies at TU/e.

## Link to ReCreate and related reuse research

The ReCreate project and related TU/e work investigate reuse of precast concrete elements. Van den Brink’s thesis is an early and highly relevant study in exactly that domain: how recovered precast elements can be redesigned into new building structures.

## Best way to describe him in a reuse-focused project

> Bart / G.J. van den Brink is relevant to reuse because his TU/e master’s research studied how recovered structural precast concrete elements from office buildings could be reused in new apartment buildings.
