# People to Add — Architecture-Related Reuse Checklist

Use this checklist for people who are specifically connected to **reuse in architecture / the built environment**, including circular construction, urban mining, design for disassembly, deconstruction, material passports, building-material reuse, and buildings as material banks.

## Strict architecture-related reuse list

- [ ] **Felix Heisel**  
  **Role:** Architect; Director, Circular Construction Lab, Cornell  
  **Reuse connection:** Circular construction, deconstruction, reclaimed building materials, structural reuse, and design for disassembly.  
  **Source:** https://labs.aap.cornell.edu/ccl/research

- [ ] **Dirk E. Hebel**  
  **Role:** Professor of Sustainable Construction, KIT  
  **Reuse connection:** Co-author of *Building from Waste* and co-designer of UMAR; recovered materials and circular construction in architecture.  
  **Source:** https://www.naibooksellers.nl/building-from-waste-recovered-materials-in-architecture-and-construction.html

- [ ] **Werner Sobek**  
  **Role:** Architect / engineer  
  **Reuse connection:** Co-designer of UMAR, a built prototype based on reusable, recyclable, or compostable building resources.  
  **Source:** https://www.wernersobek.com/projects/nest-unit-umar/

- [ ] **Annette Hillebrandt**  
  **Role:** Architect; professor, University of Wuppertal  
  **Reuse connection:** Urban Mining Design, circularity in architecture, dismantling-friendly construction, and buildings as material resources.  
  **Source:** https://urban-mining-index.de/en/initiative/

- [ ] **Petra Riegler-Floors**  
  **Role:** Professor / author  
  **Reuse connection:** Co-author of *Manual of Recycling: Buildings as Sources of Materials*, focused on circularity in architecture and urban mining design.  
  **Source:** https://toc.library.ethz.ch/objects/pdf03/e03_978-3-95553-492-9_01.pdf

- [ ] **Anja Rosen**
