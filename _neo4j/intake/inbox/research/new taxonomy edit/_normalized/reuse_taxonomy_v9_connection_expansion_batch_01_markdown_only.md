## Main changes by project
- **55 Great Suffolk Street:** Resolved reclaimed-steel external-core edges to component level, including donor source, external reuse location, hot-cut deconstruction, testing/CE marking and availability-based design.
- **BioPartner 5:** Resolved donor-steel main structure and rubble/green-facade material processing; added candidate furniture/doors component from explicit source evidence.
- **Circl:** Expanded textile-to-acoustic, jeans insulation, restored furniture, reused windows/glazing, window-frame/floor and circular procurement/reversible design edges.
- **The Green House Utrecht:** Resolved smoked-glass panels, Tiel paving bricks, pre-used wood and demountable-kit method; flagged new steel frame as future-reuse design rather than direct reuse.
- **Resource Rows:** Resolved brick modules as section reuse with external donor sources and cutting process; added candidate metro-waste-wood stream.
- **People’s Pavilion Eindhoven:** Mapped borrowed beams, concrete, facade elements and glass roof to loan/temporary-return logic; mapped plastic shingles to material reprocessing.
- **K.118 Winterthur:** Added candidate component groups for steel beams, exterior stair and granite facade, with donor/source/location/method edges.
- **Recypark Demets:** Resolved glulam arches to donor, location, integrated dismantling/storage, lab testing and reinforcement/QS edges.
- **MedUni Campus Wien:** Separated supported component-catalog mediation from unsupported reuse outcomes; flagged fluorescent tubes as dismantling-only and doors-as-cladding as weak evidence.
- **TRÆ High-Rise:** Expanded aluminium facade, turbine-blade shading, Gellerup flooring, recycled Troldtekt acoustic panels and reused window/double-glazing streams.
