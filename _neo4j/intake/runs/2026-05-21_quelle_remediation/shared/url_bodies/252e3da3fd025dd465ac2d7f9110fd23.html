Overview 

Timber Square is an ambitious new campus in the heart of Bankside. 

Offering 365,000 sq ft of striking workspace, Timber Square is designed to help businesses be the best they can be, for their people, for their purpose and for the planet. 

Set across two buildings with a central urban square - Print a 1950s printworks transformed into a 10-storey building honouring heritage by retaining the best parts of its past, and Ink, at 15 storeys, the largest hybrid building using steel and CLT in Europe. 

Timber Square is a place made differently, and a place to make a difference.
