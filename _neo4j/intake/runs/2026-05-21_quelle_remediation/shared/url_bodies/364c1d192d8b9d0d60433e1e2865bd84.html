Ivan Thung, Lead of the Circular Buildings Coalition at Metabolic breaks down the highest impact sector: the built environment. The Circular Buildings Coalition comprises six organisations working together towards a more circular, lower-impact construction sector.

2024 Open Call
10 selected winners!

In the 2024 Open Call, Circular Buildings Coalition received 57 responses to the Open Call for Blueprint Projects, from which we selected 10 initiatives to participate in the first phase of the programme. At the end of the first phase all initiatives submitted a whitepaper. On the basis of the whitepapers the selection committee selected 5 Blueprint Projects to receive additional funding and mentoring. This phase of the Open Call is guided by Metabolic Ventures. Read the whitepaper and learn more about the winning projects below.
