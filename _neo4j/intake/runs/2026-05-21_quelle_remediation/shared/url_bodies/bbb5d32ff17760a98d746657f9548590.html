    About Danish Standards

    Danish Standards sells standards, eco-labels, courses and consultancy services and promotes knowledge about standards and eco-labels – and their value. All interested parties can participate in the development of standards through our more than 230 standardization committees.

Welcome to Danish Standards

Danish Standards is Denmark’s national standardization organization, and we strive to promote standards as a strategic foundation for future solutions. We develop, publish and sell standards that improve safety, sustainability and Danish competitiveness.

Who we are

Danish Standards is Denmark’s national standardization organization, and we strive to promote standards as a strategic foundation for future solutions. We develop, publish and sell standards that improve safety, sustainability and Danish competitiveness. Our work creates a solid foundation for innovation and growth in companies and society.
