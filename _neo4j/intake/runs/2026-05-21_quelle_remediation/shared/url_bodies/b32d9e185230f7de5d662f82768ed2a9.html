    Testing and certification according to DIN EN ISO 12944: The key to effective corrosion protection for steel structures

    Significance of DIN EN ISO 12944

    DIN EN ISO 12944 is an internationally recognised set of standards that defines the principles and guidelines for the corrosion protection of steel structures by means of coating systems. Corrosion protection of steel structures is crucial because corrosion threatens the stability and service life of steel. Moisture, corrosive substances and temperature changes are some of the challenges faced by steel structures. DIN EN ISO 12944 provides clear guidelines to meet these challenges and ensure long-term corrosion protection. The standard reflects decades of experience of the expert groups involved and involved specialist companies and it therefore receives great recognition in corrosion protection.

    The sections of DIN EN ISO 12944

    The DIN EN ISO 12944 standard is divided into different sections:

    1. General Introduction and Overview

    2. Classification of environments

    3. Design considerations

    4. Types of surface and surface preparation

    5. Protective Paint Systems

    6. Laboratory performance test methods

    7. Execution and supervision of paint work

    8. Development of specifications for new work and maintenance

    9. Protective paint systems and laboratory performance test methods for offshore and related structures

    These sections cover the entire spectrum of the corrosion protection process, from surface preparation to the selection of suitable coating systems, as well as verification by laboratory test to inspection and maintenance. Part 9 covers the special application in the offshore sector and was only subsequently added to the DIN EN ISO 12944 series of standards.

    Advantages of DIN EN ISO 12944

    The correct implementation of the standard ensures a high quality of the protective coatings. This results in long-term corrosion resistance and reduces the likelihood of damage and expensive repairs. The standard provides companies with the opportunity to reduce costs by increasing the life time of steel structures and minimizing maintenance.

    Areas of application of DIN EN ISO 12944: Protection against corrosion in various branches of industry

    DIN EN ISO 12944 is applied in various areas where the corrosion protection of steel structures is of crucial importance. Some specific examples of applications of this standard are:

    1. Industrial equipment: In the petrochemical industry, power generation, food processing, and other industries where steel structures are exposed to high corrosive conditions, the standard is used to ensure the long-term protection and integrity of equipment.

    2. Construction: In bridges, high-rise buildings, tunnels, railway stations and other infrastructure projects that include steel structures, DIN EN ISO 12944 plays an important role in ensuring the safety and durability of these structures under various environmental conditions.

    3. Offshore and coastal installations: In the offshore industry, including oil and gas platforms, wind farms and other maritime structures, the standard is applied to ensure corrosion protection of steel structures in the demanding and aggressive environments of the seas.

    4. Transportation: In ships, ferry terminals, port cranes and other maritime means of transport and infrastructure, DIN EN ISO 12944 is used to ensure the protection of steel components from the effects of salt water, moisture and other corrosive factors.

    Determination of effective corrosion protection and overview of the protection period in accordance with DIN EN ISO 12944

    Corrosivity category: The right assessment for effective corrosion protection

    DIN EN ISO 12944 defines corrosivity categories that reflect the environmental conditions to which steel structures are exposed. This classification enables a targeted selection of coating systems for the appropriate corrosion protection. The classes include C1 to C5 and cover different environments, from low to high corrosiveness.

     

    Corrosivity CategoryDescription
    C1Very low corrosively (dry interiors)
    C2Low corrosiveness (damp interiors)
    C3Moderate corrosivity (urban and industrial environments)
    C4High corrosively (industrial environments with high humidity and aggressive conditions)
    C5Very high corrosiveness (aggressive atmospheres, such as coastal regions)
    CXExceptional corrosion load; environments with aggressive influences, such as offshore

    DIN EN ISO 12944 uses corrosivity categories to accurately describe environments for steel structures. The definition of the corrosivity categories comes from ISO 9223, in which the corrosivity categories are calculated according to removal rates due to corrosion on the uncoated test specimen or corrosion. The composition of the atmosphere (e.g. chloride content, or SO2 content). They make it easier to assess the corrosion load and to choose suitable protection systems. This supports the precise selection of corrosion protection measures according to the specific environmental conditions. Not every application requires the best corrosion protection system.

    Protection period of corrosion protection: Effective protection for long-term durability in accordance with DIN EN ISO 12944

    DIN EN ISO 12944 provides important guidelines for the corrosion protection of steel structures and also defines the duration of protection. This information is of great importance to ensure the effectiveness, durability and ultimately the service life of the corrosion protection systems, that they meet the requirements in different environments. The standard distinguishes between 4 different time intervals:

    Durability Time Ranges
    L - low <7 years
    M - medium 7 ≥ x < 15 years
    H - high 15 ≥ x ≤ 25 years
    VH - very high ≥ 25 years

    The term of protection is not to be understood as a warranty period, but as a planning parameter for when maintenance measures must be planned.
