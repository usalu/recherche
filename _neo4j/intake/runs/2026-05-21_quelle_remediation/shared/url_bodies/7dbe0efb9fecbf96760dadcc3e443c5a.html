Dismantling of Circl pavilion starts

April 30, 2024

On Monday, 13 May 2024, work will start on the dismantling of the Circl pavilion in Gustav Mahlerplein. It is the first stage in the renovation of the whole ABN AMRO complex. Pedestrians will experience some minor disruption over a nine-month period.

  • Business District
  • Circl
  • Public Space
Marcel Steinbach
Where?

In the years ahead, the ABN AMRO complex is being renovated by its owner Victory Group. The Circl pavilion is not suitable for expansion and is therefore being dismantled. Icon Real Estate, a division of Victory Group, will carry out the works. In the process, construction materials will be carefully stored and prepared for future reuse – fully in line with the circular philosophy that inspired the building’s design.

Working times and diversions

The works will start on Monday, 13 May 2024 and will take place between 07.00 and 19.00 on weekdays (Monday to Friday). While the works are in progress, the east side of Gustav Mahlerplein, in front of the Circl building, will be closed to pedestrians and cyclists for nine months. The walking routes to the south and rear of the building will be slightly narrowed in order to create space for the work site. Throughout this period, you will be able to make use of the pedestrian and cycle route on the west side of Gustav Mahlerplein. This will be clearly signposted.

Icon Real Estate, part of Victory Group

Removal of zebra crossing

The zebra crossing opposite the pavilion, in Gustav Mahlerlaan, will be temporarily removed. This is necessary in order to enable construction traffic to access the work site. You will still be able to use the zebra crossing to the west, around 50 m in the direction of Parnassusweg.

11 and 12 May: ‘Shub grab’

As a result of the dismantling of the Circl pavilion, the plants from the building’s gardens will also be given a new life. And you can help: during a special ‘shrub grab’ on Saturday, 11 and Sunday, 12 May, you will have an opportunity to dig up plants and take them home with you.

New office building

In June 2023, the programming came to an end in the Circl pavilion, which opened in September 2017 as a circular meeting centre at the heart of Zuidas. The Circl pavilion is being replaced by a completely new, sustainable office building with amenities. Construction is scheduled to start in the first half of 2025. The owner, Victory Group, plans to present its design to local residents and businesses in the months ahead. Of course, we will keep you informed of developments.

Further information and complaints
On 30 April 2024, local residents will receive a letter informing them about the works. You can find more information about the dismantling of the Circl pavilion here. Any complaints or reports about the dismantling works can be emailed to: circl@lcp-circular.nl. Alternatively, you can telephone 0578-692064. This number is accessible 24 hours per day and you will receive a response to your complaint or report within 24 hours. Of course, if you have any questions, you can always visit or contact us at the Zuidas-Zuidasdok Information Centre.
