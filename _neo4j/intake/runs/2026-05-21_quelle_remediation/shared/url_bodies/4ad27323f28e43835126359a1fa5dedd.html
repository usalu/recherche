material is an expression of its time in its use and as such an inspiration for new interpretations.
by demonstrating the architectural design potential we extend the lifespan of building components, save resources globally and highlight the cultural value that is already contained in the pre-used elements.

material

we evaluate buildings & material flows, quantify them and thus assess the potential on an economic, ecological and social level.

value

we understand material in and on the object on site. by sharing these discoveries new appreciation of the existing arises.

exemplify

we optimize lifespan of materials by designing the process to extend the life cycle of implemented products and existing constructions.

cycle

our projects start with an analysis of potential. conception and planning lead to an implementation strategy, which we support until it is realized, so that the highly valued impact can be felt through further use.

impact

because we view buildings as material storage even during their use, we value the quality, purity, maintenance options and dismantling of the materials and components used. we act with responsibility for several generations.

sufficiency

we see goals that can be reached via routes that have not yet been defined. to achieving them, we rely on cooperation and knowledge transfer. our success lies in our actions!
