About Thoravej 29

Copyright: Emil Basse

Thoravej 29 is a community of more than 190 ambitious people from 39 organizations working with art, social innovation, sustainability, democracy, digital innovation, and politics.
All share the mission of creating positive change and transformation in society.

We live in a country with many good intentions, but too few real changes. We can do better. We must do better.

That is why everything at Thoravej 29 is about transforming society and mobilizing those who want to—and those who can. Thoravej 29 is a committed work community, a cross-sector network and meeting place, a stage for the future, a neutral ground—and a little more.

The community is driven by a deep belief that positive change in society is possible. Here, people and organizations come together across disciplines - art, social innovation, democracy, politics, digital innovation, and sustainability o break with habitual thinking and move each other, and the society we are part of, forward.

The building itself at Thoravej 29 has undergone an extensive transformation: from an old brick structure into a platform for new thinking. Architect Søren Pihlman took a radical approach to reuse from the very beginning, an ethos that has permeated the entire renovation. The result: CO2 emissions reduced by up to 88% compared to new construction, 95% of materials reused, and 90% of all waste minimized. See more about sustainability at Thoravej 29 here.

With creativity and sustainability at its core, the former industrial building has been turned into a space designed to inspire, challenge, and foster collaboration across fields.

Thoravej 29 houses everything from workshops, exhibition and project spaces to offices, video- and sound studios, a black box, artists’ studios, a café, and an open stage for a wide range of events.
